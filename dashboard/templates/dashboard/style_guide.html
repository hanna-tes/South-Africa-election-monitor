How the 2026 Election Works

Election Day: Wednesday, 4 November 2026. Voting stations open 07:00–21:00.

  • The System: Mixed system. Half ward councillors (voted as individuals), half proportional representation (party lists).
  • The Scale: 257 municipalities, 4,485 wards, and 10,526 council seats.
  • Independents: Can contest ward seats only. 621 independent candidates certified.
  • Ballots: Metro voters get 2 ballots (ward, PR); elsewhere voters get a third ballot for the district council.
Data Coverage: This file covers 75,311 of 136,790 IEC certified candidates (54%). Ward vs PR split in file: ~38% Ward / 62% PR (IEC verified: ~72% Ward / 28% PR). 61 rows have no gender recorded. Zero social URLs mapped.
